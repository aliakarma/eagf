KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. CS1: EAGF raises Trust Index by +38.97% (0.565 -> 0.785).
   - Recall Parity improves +15.1% (0.786 -> 0.905).
   - Accountability contributes 77.6% of TI gain.
   - Accuracy cost: -6.00 pp (governance trade-off).

2. CS2: EAGF raises Trust Index by +69.3% (0.358 -> 0.606).
   - FPR Parity improves +56.4% (0.493 -> 0.771).
   - Accuracy stable: +2.6% (macro-averaged).
   - System overhead: +0.2 ms latency, +5.8 MB memory.

3. Privacy: DP-SGD (eps=2.4) reduces MIA AUC from 0.612 to 0.541 in CS1.
   Near-random in CS2 (null empirical MIA effect in tabular domain).

4. No pillar is redundant: Leave-one-out confirms every mechanism degrades TI.
   Removing accountability costs most (-0.166 TI).

5. EAGF outperforms AIF360 (TI=0.410) and Fairlearn (TI=0.437) on composite
   governance despite Fairlearn achieving higher single-pillar FPRP (0.802).
